Monotype About

A refreshed look and feel for Monotype.

During my tenure at Monotype and particularly my time as Design Lead, I was involved in many key brand evolutions. In 2024 the visual identity went through its first major overhaul since 2019, under Graham Sturt's leadership.

We refreshed and rationalised existing systems (colour, layout and wordmarks) and developed new ones for illustrations, icons, infographics and social posts.

A refreshed look and feel for Monotype.

As a Brand Designer at Monotype, I contributed to the evolution of the company's global visual identity, collaborating closely with cross-functional teams to bring the refreshed brand system to life across multiple touchpoints.

The 2024-25 brand refresh focused on refining and modernising core visual elements, including colour systems, layouts, typography, iconography, illustrations, infographics, and social media assets. My work involved translating the updated brand language into consistent, scalable design solutions across digital marketing, campaigns, and brand communications with the help of design team.
